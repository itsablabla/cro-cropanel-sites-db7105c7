CRO Page Prioritization

Where the Conversion
Leaks Are

The site's primary conversion mechanism is the coverage-check form, but it is diluted by duplicate forms and competing CTAs on the homepage and plans pages, while trust signals like reviews and guarantees are absent near pricing, undermining the confidence needed to convert. Consolidating the coverage check into a single, consistent path and placing social proof adjacent to pricing would directly increase conversion.
